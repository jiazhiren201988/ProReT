# Code of Conduct

ProReT follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.
Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, focus on scientific
and technical issues, and avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to
jiazhiren@stu.njmu.edu.cn. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with
appropriate confidentiality.

The complete Contributor Covenant is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
